    In Word Chain your goal in this game is to go from one word to another word. In order to get to the target word you need to make 1 letter changes to the current word in order to make the target word. One thing to note is that most paths for 3 letter words are possible so do not give up so easily. As the amount of letters increase the higher the chance there is for a chain to not be possible. Do not worry about making an ineffecient path as you are ranked based on time not fewest path.
